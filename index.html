01 About

I'm a Senior Software and Systems Development Engineer at Honda R&D Americas, where I work across the autonomy stack for off-road autonomous vehicles: simulation, perception and obstacle detection, navigation and path planning, and the cloud backend services that tie it all together into scalable solutions.

I also co-founded Level AI, a physical AI startup, where I led the hardware and data work: wearable camera rigs on Jetson that recorded skilled construction work from the worker's point of view, to train robots by imitation learning.

Robotics started early for me, with FIRST Robotics in high school. While still a student at Duke, I joined BotBuilt as a founding engineer and built its entire system for automated prefabricated wall panels. From there I updated robotic manufacturing at AKG before moving into autonomy at Honda. At home I run a robot arm testbed for imitation-learning policies, a GPU home lab and a small machine shop.
